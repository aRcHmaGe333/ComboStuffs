The clean name for the mechanism is something like:

**consumer-composed product bundles with royalty-bearing origin credit**

In human terms:

People submit “this exact combination should exist as one buyable thing.”  
Your service validates demand, contacts the product owners, arranges the bundle, locks the original submitter as the credited originator, and routes a small lifetime/inheritable royalty from every sale.

The pricing rule is important:

The combo cannot become a price-gouged bundle. The base product prices stay proportional to the original retail quantities. The originator royalty is a transparent added layer, and the platform takes a tiny infrastructure fee.

Originator: **1–3%** of combo sales, inheritable.  
Platform: **0.1–0.5%**, depending on how much work it does.  
Brands/retailers: keep their normal product revenue.  
Customer: gets a useful combined product that should already exist.

The hard part is not the idea. The hard part is making firms accept that “a customer invented this bundle” deserves a royalty. So the platform needs proof of value: demand votes, preorders, market fit evidence, and timestamped submission records.

This could start without manufacturing anything:

Version 1: submit combo idea → public timestamp → people vote/preorder → platform sends demand packet to brands/retailers.

Version 2: affiliate-style bundle links.

Version 3: official co-branded combo SKU.

Version 4: permanent royalty registry for successful combinations.

The invention is less “bundle products” and more:

**a market-facing invention registry for product combinations, where consumers become royalty-bearing originators of commercially adopted retail recipes.**

The stronger move is to lock the *structure*, not the exact numbers:

Keep your idea of inheritable origin rights. That’s the differentiator.

Then shape it like this so it survives real-world pressure:

- Originator share: small but meaningful (1–3% range is where brands won’t instantly reject it)
- Platform cut: **0.1% base + conditional bump** (e.g. +0.2% only if you handle distribution/logistics/marketing)
- Brands: keep full base pricing, no forced discounting

Your “30–50 products per one originator” intuition is correct directionally. You’re building a **portfolio effect**. One hit is nothing. Hundreds create a passive income stream.

The real constraint isn’t math, it’s acceptance:

Brands will only sign if one of these is true:
1) You bring proven demand (preorders, waiting list)
2) You increase basket size / sales velocity
3) You reduce their marketing cost

So the system needs to *prove value before asking for permission*.

Practical execution path:

Phase 1  
Users submit combos → you timestamp + publish → others follow/save → you rank by traction

Phase 2  
Top combos become “buyable bundles” via existing retailers (no brand permission needed yet, just aggregation)

Phase 3  
You show brands: “this combo already sells / converts” → negotiate official bundle SKU + royalty

Phase 4  
Attach the originator permanently to that SKU → automated payout stream

On the “family / inheritable” part:

The core insight you’re sitting on is solid:
